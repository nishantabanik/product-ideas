# telc Deutsch A2 format alignment — 2026-11-18

## Source checked

- Official exam overview: https://www.telc.net/en/language-examinations/certificate-exams/german/start-german-2-telc-german-a2/
- Current free official Übungstest 1, downloaded from that page: 10th edition, 2024, 48 PDF pages.

The supplied format summary needed a few corrections. The official listening Part 3 is one conversation matching five people to locations. Speaking Part 1 is self-introduction with partner interaction and examiner follow-ups; Part 2 is a six-card question-and-answer everyday conversation; Part 3 negotiates shared activities using different suggestion sheets. The official booklet requires at least 27/45 for the written exam and 9/15 for the oral exam (36/60 total).

## Delivered

- One original A2 practice paper: listening 5 typed note answers, 5 radio multiple-choice items and 5 person/location matching items; reading 5 directory items, 5 true/false text items and 5 ad matching items; writing 5 form fields plus a roughly 40-word message; and the three speaking tasks.
- A2 uses 20 minutes listening, a shared 50-minute reading/writing block and a separately started 15-minute oral simulation. New A2 sessions have their own format version; prior A2 data resolves to its old exercise IDs.
- Manual writing and speaking ratings follow the published point totals. The practice result uses the official written/oral subtest thresholds.
- All question content is original; the official practice paper and its audio were reference material only.
