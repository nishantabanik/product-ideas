# telc Deutsch B1 format alignment — 2026-11-18

## Official format checked

- telc's current [Zertifikat Deutsch / telc Deutsch B1 exam page](https://www.telc.net/en/language-examinations/certificate-exams/german/certificate-german-telc-german-b1/) lists Reading (3 parts) and Language Elements (2 parts) in a shared 90-minute block, Listening (3 parts, about 30 minutes), Writing (1 part, 30 minutes), and Speaking (3 parts, about 15 minutes) with 20 minutes of preparation.
- The current page links a free official practice test and matching audio. The downloaded practice booklet is a 2020 revised edition; its test-format table confirms the 3/2/3/1/3 part counts and 90/30/30-minute blocks. The booklet's scoring table gives 225 written points, 75 oral points and separate pass thresholds of 135 and 45.
- Official page wording: [Zertifikat Deutsch / telc Deutsch B1](https://www.telc.net/en/language-examinations/certificate-exams/german/certificate-german-telc-german-b1/).

## Delivered

- Added a new original 60-item paper: 20 reading, 20 Language Elements, 20 listening and one writing task, plus all three speaking tasks.
- Added the B1 timing stages: 90 minutes Reading/Language Elements, about 30 minutes Listening, 30 minutes Writing, then 20 minutes preparation and about 15 minutes speaking.
- Added weighted B1 objective and manual practice scoring out of 300, with separate written/oral thresholds.
- Preserved prior A1/A2 formats and old generic B1 session resolution through versioned format data.
- Interface text is available in English and German; test prompts and answer options stay in German.

## Limits

- All new prompts, answer choices, sample email and listening scripts are original. This is format-aligned practice, not an official paper or licensed telc content.
- Listening uses the site's configured synthetic voice and does not simulate the official full audio program.
- Writing and speaking self/partner ratings are estimates; a qualified teacher should review task validity, answer keys, grammar and scoring before public release.
- The official PDF's sample audio cadence may differ by item. The app follows the listening play instructions used in the official practice sample: short global statements once, longer interview twice, short notices twice.
